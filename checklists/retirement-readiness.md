# Retirement Readiness

- [ ] Users and dependent systems have a migration or closure plan.
- [ ] Data retention, archive, export, legal hold, and deletion obligations are resolved.
- [ ] Records and evidence are preserved for required periods.
- [ ] Credentials, keys, tokens, certificates, and remote access are revoked.
- [ ] Infrastructure, licenses, suppliers, and billing are closed.
- [ ] Backups and restore obligations are explicitly retained or destroyed.
- [ ] Asset inventory and service catalogue are updated.
- [ ] Final acceptance, lessons, ownership transfer, and residual risk are recorded.
