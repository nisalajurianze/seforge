# Release Readiness

- [ ] Approved scope and requirements trace to evidence.
- [ ] Functional and quality gates pass.
- [ ] Security, privacy, supply-chain, and compliance checks pass for scope.
- [ ] Database and data migrations were rehearsed and reconciled.
- [ ] Artifacts are immutable, verified, and reproducible at the required level.
- [ ] Rollback or roll-forward is tested.
- [ ] Observability, SLOs, alerts, runbooks, on-call, backup, restore, and incident response are ready.
- [ ] User, administrator, developer, support, and release documentation are current.
- [ ] Known limitations and residual risks have authorized acceptance.
- [ ] Ownership, support, maintenance, EOL, and review dates are recorded.
