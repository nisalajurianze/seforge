# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, technically rigorous community. Participation should be safe and constructive for people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Disclose uncertainty, limitations, conflicts of interest, and security concerns.
- Protect private data, credentials, and responsible disclosures.
- Accept correction and update claims when evidence changes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, credential exposure, malicious code, unsafe demonstrations, and bad-faith manipulation are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens people, project integrity, or security. Serious security matters should use the private reporting process in `SECURITY.md`.
