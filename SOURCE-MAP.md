# Source Map and Research Basis

## Source hierarchy used by the pack

1. explicit current user instruction;
2. verified project facts and repository evidence;
3. mandatory laws, contracts, certification rules, and organization policy;
4. current official standards, specifications, and platform documentation;
5. peer-reviewed or authoritative engineering literature;
6. uploaded course material as the academic foundation;
7. pack synthesis and safe defaults.

A source does not become a project requirement merely because it is listed here. Applicability must be established by project context.

## Uploaded academic foundation

The user's uploaded Software Engineering materials provide the academic baseline for:

- professional software engineering, product attributes, application types, and ethics;
- software processes, Waterfall, incremental, reuse-oriented, and Agile development;
- requirements engineering;
- design, implementation, UML, architecture, patterns, build/buy, and open source;
- testing, verification, validation, TDD, development, release, and user testing;
- software evolution, maintenance, and re-engineering;
- dependability and security;
- CASE tools;
- Ian Sommerville, *Software Engineering*, 9th edition, as the broad textbook reference.

The AI-search lecture handouts are excluded from this software-engineering pack and belong in the Artificial Intelligence profile.

## Source freshness

The v2.0 agent architecture was researched and assembled on 2026-08-22. Current standards, provider formats, security guidance, and platform behavior must be revalidated before a high-stakes decision or formal conformance claim.

## Synthesis boundary

The pack's routing logic, action-risk model, context budgets, evidence ledger, workflows, templates, and quality gates are operational synthesis. They are not a substitute for the complete applicable standard, legislation, certification assessment, or competent specialist review.
